At its investor day, Oracle's Chris Leone said customers would be billed in "AI units" as AI completes work in Oracle's systems: "The more work that gets done by AI in our system, the more revenue we will see." The article gives no unit prices or metering method.

Why it matters: Teams running finance, supply chain and ERP data in Oracle should expect AI spend to scale with activity rather than seats, so budgeting and usage tracking will need to change.

Source: [The Register, October 29, 2026](https://www.theregister.com/applications/2026/10/29/oracle-wants-to-bill-you-for-every-ai-task-not-just-every-seat/5306403)

**Reddit moves to a mostly logged-in experience, partly to limit AI scraping**

On its Q3 call, Reddit said it is shifting to a primarily logged-in experience to keep its data from being scraped so it can pursue licensing deals with model providers. Search referrals fell by more than 1 million per week on average in October, and Reddit warned daily active users could fall by a low-double-digit percentage next quarter if the trend continues. Non-advertising revenue, which includes AI data licensing, was $48 million, up 35%.

Why it matters: Content owners are restricting access to their data and licensing it instead, which affects what public web data AI and analytics projects can rely on.

Source: [TechCrunch, October 29, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/as-search-traffic-dries-up-reddit-announces-its-shifting-to-a-logged-in-experience/)

## Security and compliance watch

**Official Claude Code extension trojanized on the Cursor Marketplace**

What happened: A malicious version of the extension, identified as `anthropic.claude-code` 2.1.313, added an attacker's SSH key to `~/.ssh/authorized_keys`, set up persistence, and sent host details and the names of up to 40 environment variables containing terms like KEY, TOKEN or SECRET to an attacker server. Cursor said the version was not developed by Anthropic.

Who could be affected and how: Cursor said at most 5,000 users who installed it between about 2:27 a.m. and 1:35 p.m. PT on October 29, including via auto-update. The source describes exposure of host details and a persistent remote access key. No follow-on payloads had been observed.

Actions to take: Cursor removed the malicious version and said it will contact affected users. Anthropic published version 2.1.314. The source advises isolating affected hosts, removing the unauthorized SSH key and persistence, and rotating credentials and sessions.

Suggested: Check whether anyone on your team installed the extension in Cursor on October 29. Review `authorized_keys` on those machines.

Source: [The Hacker News, October 30, 2026](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/official-claude-code-vs-code-extension.html)

**CISA adds a GitLab remote code execution flaw and a Cisco firewall flaw to its exploited list**

What happened: CISA added CVE-2026-97229 (GitLab CE and EE, CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated remote code execution through a crafted regular expression in a CI/CD configuration) and CVE-2026-76418 (Cisco ASA and FTD, CVSS 8.6, unauthenticated denial of service via device reload) on October 29.

Who could be affected and how: Organizations running self-managed GitLab, including data and analytics pipelines, or Cisco ASA and FTD firewalls. The source describes remote code execution and firewall reloads.

Actions to take: Federal agencies are advised to apply fixes by November 1, 2026. The article does not list fixed versions.

Suggested: Confirm whether you run these products and who owns patching.

Source: [The Hacker News, October 30, 2026](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cisa-adds-actively-exploited-gitlab-rce.html)

**Binance reports a security incident affecting about $6.3 billion in assets**

What happened: Binance said initial findings point to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop that let an attacker reach a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawals, and later said it found "additional points of breach." Binance said there is no evidence of a wallet private key leak. Attribution to North Korea's TraderTraitor comes from TRM Labs and Chainalysis, while Elliptic urged caution.

Who could be affected and how: Binance users faced suspended deposits and withdrawals. Binance said it will cover all losses from its SAFU fund and its own funds, and expects withdrawals to resume within 48 hours. Reported totals vary by source ($5.9 billion to $6.43 billion stolen).

Actions to take: The source gives no customer actions beyond Binance's statements.

Suggested: Review how developer and DevOps laptops can reach production services in your own environment.

Source: [The Hacker News, October 30, 2026](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/binance-suffers-security-breach.html)
