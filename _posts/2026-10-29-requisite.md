## AI products and models

**Anthropic adds spend-versus-value reports and pooled budgets for enterprise teams**

Anthropic announced reporting tools that draw on internal analytics and employee surveys. Managers can review a team's work over a date range, including what it produced, what it cost and where workflows broke down. Anthropic says it will not use this data for model training by default.

Why it matters: Clients being asked to justify AI spend now have a vendor-supplied way to tie cost to team output, though the reports are the vendor's own view.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/anthropic-just-launched-tools-to-show-companies-what-their-ai-spending-is-buying/) (Oct 28, 2026)

**Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon to security defenders first, priced to match Claude Opus 5.5 after an introductory period**

Google calls Carbon its strongest model yet, especially for cybersecurity and long-running tasks. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for an undisclosed period, rising to $4 and $20. Access is limited to an unspecified set of cybersecurity defenders for now. The Register notes most performance figures come from Google's own benchmarks or public leaderboards, and Carbon is fourth on WebDev Arena.

Why it matters: Teams budgeting for model costs should plan around the post-introductory price, not the launch price.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/29/googles-gemini-4-carbon-looks-like-a-diamond-in-the-rough/5306149) (Oct 29, 2026)

**Google opens Gemini and Search to third-party apps through a Connected Apps Platform**

Businesses and developers can connect apps to Search and Gemini through a cloud-hosted MCP server or Android MCP. Gemini already has over 50 connected apps, which are coming to Search over the next few weeks. The platform is in public preview for U.S. developers on a waitlist, with no general availability date.

Why it matters: Companies that expect customers to use AI assistants will need to decide whether and how their services appear inside them.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/as-ai-agents-reshape-the-web-google-opens-gemini-and-search-to-more-apps/) (Oct 28, 2026)

## Data and analytics industry

**Databricks makes serverless GPU for clean room notebooks generally available**

Clean room notebooks can now run on serverless GPU compute for model inference and training over shared data. They use the same approval flow, data access and output tables as other clean room notebooks.

Why it matters: Partners can train or score models on jointly held data without moving it, using the governance controls clean rooms already apply.

Source: [Databricks release notes, October 2026](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/release-notes/product/2026/october) (Oct 28, 2026)

## AI and the workforce

**Infosys says AI savings are now priced into client contracts, and its growth guidance has dropped**

Infosys's AI revenue grew 29% sequentially and is 10.5% of incoming cash, but it cut full-year revenue growth guidance from 1.5 to 3.0% to 1.0 to 2.0%. Its CFO said customers expect lower prices when AI is used and those savings are built into multi-year deals.

Why it matters: Service firms and their clients are renegotiating who keeps the productivity gain from AI, which affects consulting rates and staffing models.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/channel/2026/10/28/ai-slows-growth-for-infosys-and-keeps-it-afloat-at-the-same-time/5305879) (Oct 28, 2026)

**Amazon tells engineers to measure value, not token use**

An Amazon memo made public on October 27 says input metrics such as AI usage are "disconnected from what matters" and could raise costs and complexity if used as targets. It says engineers who find AI does not help their work should explain why, calling that "a finding, not a failing." The Register notes Microsoft took a similar position in August.

Why it matters: Usage dashboards can reward the wrong behavior, so leaders need outcome measures for AI adoption.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/28/amazon-tells-tokenmaxxing-devs-to-slow-their-ai-roll/5306090) (Oct 28, 2026)

**An Anthropic engineering manager says he has stopped using coding interviews, while a ZipRecruiter survey finds most employers still test**

Felix Rieseberg wrote that AI tools now outperform candidates on small coding challenges. ZipRecruiter's October survey of 1,500 employers found 62.5% of Information-sector employers require formal skill tests, and 70% of those have moved to proctored or in-person assessments to deter AI cheating.

Why it matters: Hiring teams are split on how to assess skills when AI can pass the standard tests.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/28/anthropic-engineer-declares-the-coding-interview-dead/5306115) (Oct 28, 2026)

## Off the beaten path

**Field experiment: human-AI teams made more ads and better text, but more similar output (arXiv preprint, revised October 15)**

Harang Ju and Sinan Aral randomly assigned 2,234 participants to human-human or human-AI teams that produced 11,024 ads. Human-AI teams produced about 50% more ads per worker and higher text quality, while human-human teams produced higher image quality. The authors report "diversity collapse" (more self-similar ads) in human-AI teams. The paper is an arXiv preprint (version 4); I could not confirm peer review from the page, and the results come from one platform and one ad-creation task.

Why it matters: Where AI is added to creative or content teams, output volume may rise while variety falls, so measure both.

Source: [arXiv:2503.18238](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18238) (v1 March 2025, v4 Oct 15, 2026)

## Environmental and societal impacts

**San Diego City Council unanimously approves a 45-day data center moratorium**

The vote followed a proposed data center at the NAVWAR site near Old Town. The council could extend the pause, and staff have 180 days to submit preliminary recommendations. KESQ notes NAVWAR is federally owned, so the moratorium is largely symbolic for that site.

Why it matters: Clients planning data center capacity or relying on cloud regions in affected areas should expect local permitting to become a schedule risk.

Source: [KESQ](https://kesq.com/news/california/2026/10/27/san-diego-city-council-unanimously-passes-moratorium-on-data-centers/) (Oct 27, 2026)

**Shareholders push Microsoft to disclose how it will meet its 2030 emissions targets**

Trillium Asset Management is backing a proposal for an annual disclosure of the plan. Microsoft's reported emissions rose from 13 million tons of CO2 equivalent in 2020 to 20 million tons in 2025, 62% above the 2020 baseline. Microsoft recommends a vote against, and its shareholder meeting is scheduled for December 8.

Why it matters: Investors are asking how AI growth squares with climate commitments, a question clients' own boards may start to ask.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/10/28/microsoft-faces-potential-vote-shareholders-curious-about-its-plans-to-tackle-emissions/5305953) (Oct 28, 2026)

## Security and compliance watch

**123 Reg discloses an "AI-driven" attack that read customer data**

What happened: The UK domain registrar, owned by GoDaddy, says an attacker accessed customer contact data, VAT numbers and product information from an API on September 18. Activity was described as limited to reading data, though copies may have been made. 123 Reg suspects a Chinese state-linked group, which is a suspicion and not a confirmed finding.

Who could be affected and how: 123 Reg customers, who were notified by email. Exposed details could be used for targeted phishing.

Actions to take: 123 Reg advises treating unexpected messages claiming to be from it with caution, logging in directly through its website, and enabling two-step verification. It says it will never ask for passwords, payment details or verification codes by email, text or phone. Suggested: check whether your organization's domains are registered with 123 Reg and warn staff about phishing.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/28/123-reg-discloses-ai-cyberattack-on-customer-data-suspects-china-nexus/5305936) (Oct 28, 2026)

**26,400 working cloud and AI credentials found in public Gitee repositories**

What happened: Researcher Ben Zimmermann scanned 7.25 million public Gitee repositories and found 26,400 valid credentials, including 9,473 Alibaba Cloud AccessKey pairs, 3,170 DeepSeek keys, 1,348 OpenAI keys and one active organization-admin credential for an unnamed enterprise AI platform. He notified vendors but not the developers, and had not confirmed any were revoked.

Who could be affected and how: Anyone who has ever committed a key to a public Gitee repository, with risk of unauthorized use and unexpected charges. It is unclear whether anyone else has used the keys.

Actions to take: The article says to treat any key that has been in a public Gitee repository as exposed, since deleting it or rewriting history does not revoke it. Rotate Alibaba Cloud AccessKeys and check for unexpected charges. Rotate Google or YouTube keys and add API restrictions. Rotate OpenAI keys and set spend limits. Suggested: check whether any staff or contractors use Gitee.

Source: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/26400-valid-cloud-and-ai-credentials.html) (Oct 29, 2026)

**Oracle E-Business Suite SQL injection is being probed, and a PeopleSoft zero-day has been exploited by ShinyHunters**

What happened: Horizon3 disclosed a critical unauthenticated SQL injection in Oracle E-Business Suite (CVE-2026-101767) that can lead to remote code execution, fixed in Oracle's October 2026 Critical Patch Update. Defused Cyber recorded exploratory attempts on October 27. Separately, Mandiant saw mass exploitation of a PeopleSoft zero-day (CVE-2026-35273), with web shells on dozens of systems.

Who could be affected and how: Organizations running EBS or PeopleSoft. Horizon3 says EBS data may include financial, payroll, HR and procurement records. The article did not specify affected versions.

Actions to take: Apply the October 2026 EBS patches and the PeopleSoft patch for CVE-2026-35273. Check systems for web shells, since patching may not evict attackers already inside, and restrict localhost and trusted-host access to PSEMHUB and PSIGW. Suggested: confirm who owns patching for these systems.

Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/28/oracle-celebrates-end-of-cybersecurity-awareness-month-with-quadruple-trouble/5306121) (Oct 28, 2026)
